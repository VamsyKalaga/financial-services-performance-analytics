# KPI Definitions

## Total Customers

Total number of customers registered in the customer dataset.

## Active Customers

Number of unique customers who completed at least one transaction.

## Customer Activity Rate

Percentage of total customers who completed at least one transaction.

## Total Transactions

Total number of transactions recorded in the system.

## Completed Transactions

Number of transactions with a Completed status.

## Failed Transactions

Number of transactions with a Failed status.

## Pending Transactions

Number of transactions with a Pending status.

## Total Revenue

Total transaction amount from completed transactions.

## Average Transaction Value

Average transaction amount across completed transactions.

## Transaction Success Rate

Percentage of all transactions with a Completed status.

## Transaction Failure Rate

Percentage of all transactions with a Failed status.

## Transactions per Active Customer

Average number of completed transactions generated per active customer.

## Revenue per Active Customer

Total completed transaction revenue divided by the number of active customers.

## Month-over-Month Revenue Growth

Percentage change in revenue compared with the previous month.
