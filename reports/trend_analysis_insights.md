# Trend Analysis Insights

## Analysis Period

January 2024 through December 2025.

## Key Performance Metrics

- Average monthly transaction value: approximately $1.02M
- Average monthly transactions: approximately 2,778
- Average monthly active customers: approximately 1,862
- Average transaction value: approximately $367.15

## Transaction Value Trends

- June 2024 recorded the highest monthly transaction value at approximately $1.41M.
- October 2024 recorded approximately $1.22M.
- December 2025 recorded approximately $1.15M.
- August 2025 recorded the lowest monthly transaction value at approximately $839.85K.

## Growth Analysis

### Largest Month-over-Month Increases

- June 2024: 57.88%
- October 2024: 35.78%
- October 2025: 14.67%
- July 2025: 11.48%
- December 2025: 9.26%

### Largest Month-over-Month Declines

- July 2024: -31.66%
- August 2025: -21.26%
- April 2024: -13.14%
- January 2025: -12.92%
- May 2024: -9.88%

## Customer Activity

Monthly active customers remained relatively stable compared with the larger fluctuations observed in transaction value.

December 2025 recorded 1,924 active customers and approximately $1.15M in transaction value.

## Customer Value Analysis

The value-per-active-customer metric was calculated to provide additional context beyond total transaction value.

December 2025 recorded approximately $595.34 in transaction value per active customer.

## Business Interpretation

The analysis demonstrates how monthly transaction performance can be evaluated using:

- Monthly aggregation
- Month-over-month growth
- Year-over-year growth
- Rolling averages
- Active customer trends
- Transaction value per active customer

The analysis identifies periods of significant growth and decline but does not attribute those changes to specific business causes because the synthetic dataset does not contain explanatory business-event fields.
